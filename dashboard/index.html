About SafeGuard

SafeGuard is a safety-monitoring project with the goal of combining sensor inputs to help identify potential dangers and alert the right people sooner, especially when someone may be panicked or unable to call for help. Faster alerts could support a quicker response, but cannot guarantee safety or replace contacting emergency services directly. This prototype currently includes device code that listens for gunshot-like sounds and can publish a detection to AWS IoT Core when configured. This dashboard is not connected to that device or AWS: its alerts and activity graph use simulated data, and it does not contact first responders. A detection is only a possible alert and needs human verification. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
