About SafeGuard

SafeGuard is a safety-monitoring project with the goal of combining sensor inputs to help identify potential dangers and alert the right people sooner, especially when someone may be panicked or unable to call for help. Faster alerts could support a quicker response, but cannot guarantee safety or replace contacting emergency services directly. This prototype currently includes device code that listens for gunshot-like sounds and can publish a detection to AWS IoT Core when configured. The dashboard's sample alerts and activity graph use simulated data. Its Detection tab can control local detector scripts; audio alert metadata may be published to AWS IoT when configured, while camera images are sent only to the local image detector. It does not contact first responders. Model results are possible detections and need human verification. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
